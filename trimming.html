Why Does Lawn Trimming Work Differently in New Orleans?

Lawn trimming works differently in New Orleans because our long, humid growing season keeps grass growing fast from spring through fall, so edges grow out faster than in cooler climates. Trees need careful work around the trunk, and heavy rains can wash loose clippings into the street and storm drains.

  • Edges grow out fast.Spring and summer growth means more frequent trimming to keep borders sharp.
  • Trees need a buffer.LSU AgCenter warns that trimmer line can strip bark from young or thin-barked trees, so we keep it off the trunk.
  • Clippings stay out of the drains.LSU AgCenter advises picking up clippings that could clog storm drains, so we clear them off curbs and walks.

FAQ

What Do People Ask About Lawn Trimming in New Orleans?

what is trimming in lawn care?

Trimming in lawn care means cutting the grass a mower cannot reach, such as along fences and walls, around trees and posts, and beside beds. It is done right after mowing, so the whole lawn ends up at an even height with a clean, finished outline instead of shaggy strips at the borders.

what is edging in lawn care?

Lawn edging is cutting a clean line where the lawn meets a hard surface or a bed, such as a sidewalk, driveway, curb, or garden border. It stops grass from creeping over the edge, and together with trimming it gives a freshly mowed New Orleans lawn the sharp outline that makes it look finished.

what is the purpose of edging a lawn?

Edging keeps grass from spreading over walks, drives, and beds, and it gives the lawn a clean outline. Overgrown edges make the whole yard look untidy, even right after a mow. In New Orleans, where edges grow out fast from spring through fall, regular edging keeps the borders sharp between visits.

how often should you edge your lawn?

In New Orleans, edging is usually done along with mowing, so the edges keep pace with the grass. That often means every 5 to 7 days in spring and weekly in summer, slowing down in fall. We generally recommend weekly or bi-weekly service during the peak growing season to keep borders sharp.

how to trim lawn edges?

Mow first, then use an edger or trimmer to clean up the borders the mower missed. Near beds and shrubs, LSU AgCenter suggests moving the trimmer slowly from the outside in, so the line reaches the weeds without cutting into the plants. Wear eye protection and long sleeves and pants while you work.

can you use a string trimmer to mow lawn?

A string trimmer can cut a small patch, but it is a poor substitute for a mower on a whole lawn. It is hard to hold an even height, and it is easy to scalp the grass, which exposes bare soil and invites weeds. Trimmers work best on the edges and tight spots a mower cannot reach.

how to trim grass under fence?

Work slowly along the base of the fence, letting the trimmer line reach the grass without striking posts, boards, or nearby plants. Where the line cannot reach cleanly, LSU AgCenter recommends hand tools for precise close-quarter work. Clear the clippings afterward, so they do not wash into the street or storm drains.
