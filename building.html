Local Conditions

Why Does Landscape Installation Work Differently in New Orleans?

Landscape installation works differently in New Orleans because intense heat, high humidity, and heavy Gulf rains test every outdoor project, and many local soils drain slowly. LSU AgCenter puts improving internal drainage first when preparing beds, so a new landscape here is planned around water before plants are chosen.

  • Drainage comes first.Where soil stays wet, LSU AgCenter suggests a raised bed, a planting mound, or a French drain.
  • Clay needs a transition.Mixing about 3 inches of new soil into heavy clay keeps water from pooling under added soil.
  • Fall is planting season.LSU AgCenter calls October through mid-December the best time to plant trees, shrubs, and ground covers in Louisiana.
Build Elements

What Goes Into a New Landscape Installation in New Orleans?

A new landscape installation in New Orleans usually comes down to drainage that moves heavy rain away, irrigation that waters deeply instead of often, and lighting for paths and features after dark. LSU AgCenter adds planting beds raised above wet soil, which it calls almost essential for establishing landscape plants on poorly drained ground.

Drainage

First priority

LSU AgCenter names internal drainage the first priority in bed preparation. French drains carry water away from poorly drained planting sites.

Planting Beds

6 to 8 in raised

On poorly drained ground, LSU AgCenter recommends raised beds at least 6 to 8 inches deep, or a 1 foot mound for a single tree or shrub.

Irrigation

Deep, weekly

In dry spells, new trees and shrubs should be watered deeply about once a week, ideally in the morning or evening.

Lighting

12 volt

LSU AgCenter notes most home landscape lights run at 12 volts through a transformer, with path lights and well lights for walks and beds.

Bed, watering, and lighting guidance follows LSU AgCenter. Well-prepared, mulched beds hold their soil in heavy rain.

When Is the Best Time for Landscape Installation in New Orleans?

The best time for landscape installation in New Orleans is fall, from October through mid-December, according to LSU AgCenter. Roots grow through our mild winters while tops rest, so new trees and shrubs are established before summer heat arrives. Spring and summer projects work too, with closer watering.

FAQ

What Do People Ask About Landscape Installation in New Orleans?

what is landscape installation?

Landscape installation is the work of turning a plan into a finished yard, which can include drainage, irrigation, and lighting. In New Orleans, the job also means planning for heat, humidity, and heavy rain so the new landscape holds up season after season. LSU AgCenter advises making internal drainage the first priority when preparing planting beds.

how much does it cost to landscape a yard?

The cost to landscape a yard in New Orleans depends on the size of the project, the materials involved, and the scope of work, such as whether drainage, irrigation, or lighting is part of it. The most accurate number comes from a free on-site estimate, which we provide with no obligation.

how do you prepare a yard for landscaping?

Start by clearing weeds and debris and watching where rain stands, since LSU AgCenter puts internal drainage first. A soil test shows pH and nutrients, and most Louisiana ornamentals do best at a pH of 5.5 to 6.5. Where soil stays wet, raise beds at least 6 to 8 inches or plant on a mound.

how often should you water new landscape plants?

New plants need regular water until they are established, which LSU AgCenter says can take two growing seasons. During extended dry spells, water newly planted trees and shrubs deeply about once a week rather than lightly every day, and water in the morning or evening, when it is cooler than midday.

how long does landscape installation take?

How long landscape installation takes depends on the size of the property and the scope of work, such as whether drainage, irrigation, or lighting is part of the job. We look at the site during the free on-site estimate and explain the scope before any work starts, so you know what is planned.

how do you landscape a front yard?

Begin with how the front yard drains and how people walk to the door, then lay out beds and a clear path before choosing plants. In New Orleans, pick plants for the sun or shade the yard actually gets, plant trees and shrubs in fall when possible, and finish the beds with mulch.

how do you landscape a new construction home?

Start with the soil and the water before choosing plants. Have the soil tested, since the LSU AgCenter lab reports pH and nutrients, and watch where rain stands around the new home. Then mix about 3 inches of new soil into heavy clay, raise beds where water sits, and plant trees and shrubs in fall.

how does landscape lighting installation work?

Most home landscape lighting runs at 12 volts, according to LSU AgCenter. A transformer steps standard 120 volt power down to 12 volts, then cable runs out to each fixture, such as path lights along walks or well lights set level with the ground so mowers can pass over them.
