FAQ

What Do People Ask About Lawn Care Under Pines in Abita Springs?

People asking about lawn care under pines in Abita Springs mostly want to know what to do with the needles, which grass will take the shade, whether pine straw invites termites, and how soil pH and lime affect centipede. These answers draw on LSU AgCenter guidance for Louisiana yards.

what ph should my lawn be?

It depends on the grass. LSU AgCenter says centipede does well at a pH of 5.5 to 6.0, while St. Augustine prefers 6.0 to 7.0. Acid soil causes more gardening problems in Louisiana than any other soil factor, so a lab soil test is the only way to pin down where an Abita Springs yard stands.

what grass grows in shade?

Among Louisiana lawn grasses, LSU AgCenter rates St. Augustine as the most shade tolerant, with Palmetto one of the stronger varieties. Centipede handles light shade but declines in heavy shade, and under thick pine cover in Abita Springs, where grass gets less than four hours of direct sun, mulch or a ground cover usually does better.

does pine straw attract termites?

Pine straw is the least likely common mulch to draw termites, because LSU AgCenter notes it is the lowest in cellulose and a good choice around foundations. Placement still matters: keep any mulch pulled back 8 to 12 inches from the slab or piers so it cannot bridge the soil treatment around the house.

is pine straw better than wood mulch?

For beds against the house, pine straw is often the better pick. LSU AgCenter calls it excellent and lowest in cellulose, while wood chips should stay away from foundations because termites feed on them. Under Abita Springs pines, start with about 3 inches of straw around shrubs and trees, then top it up yearly.

do pine needles kill grass?

A thick layer of needles left on a lawn can shade out and smother the grass beneath it. LSU AgCenter advises removing leaf litter from shaded turf regularly to avoid that extra smothering. In Abita Springs, the simple fix is to rake or blow needles off the lawn and reuse them as mulch in the beds.

how to grow grass under pine trees?

Give it as much light as you can: LSU AgCenter suggests removing lower limbs and thinning the canopy, mowing St. Augustine at 3 inches or more and centipede at 2 inches, fertilizing moderately, and keeping traffic and leaf litter off the turf. Pines compete less for soil moisture than hardwoods, so light is usually the limit.

why is my centipede grass turning yellow?

Soil pH is one cause to rule out. LSU AgCenter notes that centipede leaves can yellow when soil turns too basic, and it lists 5.5 to 6.0 as the pH range centipede prefers, so lime added without a soil test can overshoot. A lab test shows whether pH is behind the color in your Abita Springs lawn.

should I lime my lawn?

Only if a soil test says so. LSU AgCenter notes that many Louisiana soils need lime every three to five years, but the right call depends on your grass and your test results. Centipede is the one to be careful with, since lime it does not need can raise pH too far and turn the blades yellow.
