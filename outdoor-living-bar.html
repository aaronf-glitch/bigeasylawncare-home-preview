Local Conditions

How Do Heat and Rain Affect an Outdoor Bar in New Orleans?

In New Orleans, an outdoor bar faces intense heat, humidity, and heavy Gulf rains all year. Any part of the bar that stays wet invites decay and termites, so shade, drainage, and keeping wood off damp soil shape the plan from the very first visit.

  • Rot needs wet wood.LSU AgCenter says decay fungi grow on wood above 20 percent moisture, the point where it feels wet to the touch.
  • Moisture draws termites.LSU AgCenter notes termites are attracted to and sustained by wet materials, so moisture control comes first.
  • Treated wood resists both.Copper-based pressure-treated wood can be used outdoors and in ground contact, according to LSU AgCenter.

FAQ

What Do People Ask About Outdoor Bars in New Orleans?

how to build an outdoor bar?

Building an outdoor bar starts with the plan rather than the materials. Pick a spot with shade and firm ground that drains, decide how many people it should serve, and choose a counter and base that handle sun and rain. In New Orleans, keep wood off wet soil and check city permit rules first.

how to build an outdoor bar with roof?

An outdoor bar with a roof is usually set under a pergola or other cover, so guests get shade and some shelter from rain. Plan the cover and the bar together, so posts, seating, and drainage line up. In New Orleans, ask the city whether a covered structure on your lot needs a permit.

what to use for outdoor bar top?

Use a bar top that copes with direct sun, heavy rain, spills, and humidity without staying wet. Whatever the surface, it should shed water and wipe clean easily. In New Orleans, where storms soak anything left outside, a top that dries quickly lasts longer, and we go over the options at the free consultation.

how to build a stone outdoor bar?

A stone outdoor bar is heavy, so it needs firm, level ground beneath it that drains well. Plan where the counter, seating, and any shade will go before setting any stone. In New Orleans, choose a spot where rain runs off instead of pooling around the base, and check permit rules with the city.

how to build a wooden outdoor bar?

Build a wooden outdoor bar from wood rated for outdoor use. LSU AgCenter says copper-based pressure-treated wood can be used outdoors and in ground contact, and that decay fungi grow only above 20 percent wood moisture. In New Orleans, that means lifting the bar off wet soil and letting rain drain away quickly.

what outdoor bar heights work best?

The best outdoor bar height depends on how guests will use it. A bar where people stand or sit on tall stools needs a higher counter than one paired with shorter counter stools, so choose the seating first and set the counter to match it. We settle the height with you at the free consultation.

what is the best material for an outdoor bar?

The best material for an outdoor bar in New Orleans is one that holds up when it gets wet. LSU AgCenter notes that termites are attracted to and sustained by wet materials, so the frame and base should resist moisture and insects, and the bar should sit where rain drains away fast.

how much does it cost to build an outdoor bar?

The cost to build an outdoor bar depends on its size, the materials, whether it sits under a cover, and what it is paired with, such as a kitchen or seating area. The most accurate number for a New Orleans yard comes from a free on-site estimate, which we provide with no obligation.
