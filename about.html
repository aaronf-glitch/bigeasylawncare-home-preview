Local Knowledge

Why Does Local Lawn Care Experience Matter in New Orleans?

New Orleans lawns live with intense heat, high humidity, and heavy Gulf rains, so a schedule that works elsewhere often fails here. We plan every cut, trim, and cleanup around the local climate and soil, which is why the results hold up season after season instead of fading by midsummer.

  • Grass grows fast here.In spring a New Orleans lawn usually needs mowing every 5 to 7 days, and summer heat and storms push Bermuda even harder.
  • Shade is everywhere.Yards under live oaks suit St. Augustine, one of the best warm-season grasses for shade, which can be kept at 3 to 3.5 inches.
  • Rain changes the plan.LSU AgCenter advises against mowing wet grass, so after a storm we wait until the lawn has dried.
