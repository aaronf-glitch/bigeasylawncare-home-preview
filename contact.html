FAQ

What Do People Ask Before Contacting a New Orleans Lawn Care Company?

How do I get a free lawn care estimate?

Send the free estimate form on this page or call us at (504) 267-2333. We set up a free consultation, look at your lawn, talk through what you want, and give you a detailed estimate for lawn care, landscaping, or outdoor living work with no obligation to book.

What should I include when I request an estimate?

Include your name, the best phone number or email to reach you, the property address, and a short note on the work you have in mind, such as regular mowing, a cleanup, or a new patio. The more we know up front, the more useful the first conversation is.

Is the lawn care consultation really free?

Yes. The consultation and the estimate are free, and there is no obligation to book. We look at the property, explain what we would recommend and why, and give you a clear quote, so you can decide on your own schedule whether the work makes sense for you.

Where is Big Easy Lawn Care located?

Big Easy Lawn Care is based at 3436 Magazine Street, Suite 414-I, New Orleans, LA 70115. From there our crews cover the greater New Orleans area, from Metairie, Kenner, Harahan, River Ridge, and Gretna to Covington, Mandeville, Madisonville, and Abita Springs across the lake.

Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes. We provide lawn care and landscaping for both residential and commercial properties in New Orleans. Use the same form or phone number to request an estimate for a business, and include the size of the site and how often you would like service so we can plan the visit.

Can I book a single visit instead of a plan?

Yes. You can book a one-time job, such as mowing and weed-eating for an overgrown yard or a seasonal cleanup with hauling, or set up an ongoing maintenance program. Many customers book one visit first and decide on a regular schedule once they have seen the work.

How do payments work after I book?

Everything is managed from your phone or web browser. Payments are made online, and you can reserve more services whenever your lawn needs them, without waiting on a callback. It keeps lawn care simple for busy homeowners and for property managers who look after more than one site.

What if I am not happy with the work?

Tell us. We stand behind our work with a satisfaction guarantee, and if you are not happy with a visit we will make it right. We would always rather hear about a problem early than have you live with a result that does not meet the standard you booked.
