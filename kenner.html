Signs to Watch

When Should You Call a Lawn Service in Kenner?

Call a lawn service in Kenner when yellow-brown patches keep spreading through a sunny lawn, when one part of the yard stays soft and wet days after a storm, or when every mowing now takes off far more than a third of the blade. Each problem grows faster in warm weather.

Spreading Yellow-Brown Patches

LSU AgCenter notes that southern chinch bugs favor full sun, thick thatch, and hot, dry weather, and their feeding leaves patches that can merge into one dead area.

Ground That Stays Soft

A spot that squishes underfoot long after the rain stops is a drainage question, not a mowing one. Cutting it while wet leaves ruts and tears the turf.

A Lawn Past Its Height

Taking off more than a third of the blade at once stresses the grass. When the lawn is that far ahead, it needs to come down over more than one cut.

FAQ

What Do Kenner, LA Lawn Owners Want to Know?

Kenner, LA lawn owners usually want to know which mowing mistakes to avoid, what monthly service involves, who fixes a yard that holds water, and how to deal with chinch bugs and brown patch. Others ask about centipede and zoysia, two grasses common in Louisiana lawns.

what are common lawn care mistakes to avoid?

LSU AgCenter guidance points to a few repeat mistakes: cutting off more than one-third of the leaf blade in one mowing, and feeding high-nitrogen fertilizer in fall, which pushes lush growth that is more prone to cold injury. On low Kenner lots, mowing soggy ground is another, because it leaves ruts in the turf.

how much is lawn service per month?

A monthly figure depends on how often the lawn is cut, the size of the lot, and whether trimming, weed cutting, mulching, or hauling are part of the plan. For a Kenner property, the reliable number comes after we see the yard, which is why every quote starts with a free, no-obligation consultation.

who to call for drainage problems in yard?

For water that collects in the yard itself, call a landscaping company that handles drainage, which is part of our landscaping work. Most of Kenner lies at or below sea level, and the city asks residents to keep catch basins clean so water can drain from yards and streets.

what are chinch bugs?

Southern chinch bugs are small lawn insects, about the size of an ant, with black bodies and white wings that form an X. LSU AgCenter says they mainly attack St. Augustine grass, sucking sap from stems and stolons until patches turn yellow-brown, most often during hot, dry weather in mid- to late summer.

how to treat brown patch fungus?

LSU AgCenter now calls the disease large patch on warm-season grasses and recommends at least two fungicide applications when it is present, in mid- to late September and again in mid- to late October. It starts where trees, shrubs, and fences slow drying, so improving airflow in fenced Kenner backyards helps too.

what is centipede grass?

Centipede grass is a warm-season turfgrass grown widely in Louisiana for its low maintenance needs. LSU AgCenter describes it as medium textured, with sharply creased, bright green leaves that spread by aboveground stems called stolons. It performs best in full sun, prefers acidic soil, and is mowed at 1 to 2 inches.

does zoysia grass grow in shade?

Zoysia handles partial shade fairly well, and LSU AgCenter ranks it second only to St. Augustine among warm-season grasses for shade tolerance, rating it medium to excellent by variety. Most turfgrasses still need four to six hours of full sun, so in shaded Kenner yards, mowing slightly higher helps.

when to fertilize centipede grass?

LSU AgCenter advises feeding centipede grass from midspring to midsummer, with no more than 1 pound of nitrogen per 1,000 square feet per year. Too much fertilizer is a common problem with this grass, so a soil test before you buy anything is the best first step for a Kenner lawn.
