10 Lawn Care Tips for Your Yard This Spring

April 4, 2023·7 min read·By the Big Easy Lawn Care team

The Top 10 Lawn Care Tips for a Beautiful Yard This Spring

A lush, green lawn is a hallmark of a beautiful home, but achieving and maintaining it takes some effort. As the weather warms up and spring approaches, it’s time to start thinking about your lawn care routine.

In this blog, we’ll provide you with the top 10 lawn care tips for a beautiful yard this spring. From raking and removing debris to hiring a professional lawn care service, these tips will help you achieve a healthy, vibrant lawn that you can be proud of. So let’s get started!

Tip #1: Rake and Remove Debris

If you want a beautiful lawn, it’s important to start by raking and removing debris. This includes fallen leaves, sticks, and any other debris that has accumulated over the winter months.

Here’s what you need to know:

  • Why it’s important: Raking and removing debris is essential because it allows sunlight and air to reach your grass, which is necessary for healthy growth. If you leave debris on your lawn, it can block sunlight and air and create a breeding ground for pests and diseases.
  • How to do it: Start by raking your lawn thoroughly, using a sturdy rake that won’t damage your grass. Be sure to remove all of the debris you find, and dispose of it properly. If you have a large lawn, consider using a leaf blower to speed up the process.

Tip #2: Aerate Your Lawn

Aerating your lawn is a crucial step in maintaining a healthy and attractive lawn. It involves creating small holes in your lawn to allow air, water, and nutrients to penetrate deep into the soil.

When the soil in your lawn becomes compacted, it becomes difficult for essential elements to reach the roots of your grass, and aeration helps to loosen up the soil, allowing these elements to reach the roots more easily.

You can use a manual or gas-powered aerator for this task, and it’s important to water your lawn before and after aerating to ensure the soil can absorb moisture. By aerating your lawn, you’ll be promoting stronger and healthier grass, which will enhance the beauty of your lawn.

Tip #3: Fertilize Your Lawn

Fertilizing your lawn is an important part of lawn care, as it provides your grass with the essential nutrients it needs to grow healthy and strong.

Here’s what you need to know:

  • When to do it: It’s best to fertilize your lawn in the spring and fall, when your grass is growing actively. However, the exact timing will depend on the type of grass you have and your location. Check with your local gardening center or a lawn care professional for specific recommendations.
  • How to do it: You can apply fertilizer by spreading it evenly over your lawn using a spreader. Be sure to follow the instructions on the fertilizer package carefully, as over-fertilizing can damage your lawn.

Tip #4: Water Your Lawn Correctly

Proper watering is crucial for maintaining a healthy, beautiful lawn.

How Much Water

Your lawn needs about 1 inch of water per week, either from rain or from irrigation. To determine how much water your lawn is getting, you can place an empty can or container on your lawn while you water it, and measure the amount of water in the can after you finish.

When to Water

It’s best to water your lawn in the morning, as this allows the water to penetrate the soil before it evaporates in the heat of the day. Avoid watering in the evening, as this can promote the growth of fungi and other pests.

How to Water

It’s best to water deeply and infrequently, rather than shallowly and frequently. This encourages the roots of your grass to grow deeper, which can make it more drought-resistant. Use a sprinkler or irrigation system to water your lawn evenly.

Tip #5: Mow Your Lawn Properly

Mowing your lawn is an essential part of lawn care, but it’s important to do it correctly to avoid damaging your grass.

  • How often to mow: It’s best to mow your lawn regularly to keep it looking neat and tidy. However, you should never remove more than one-third of the blade of grass at a time, as this can damage your lawn.
  • How high to mow: The height at which you mow your lawn depends on the type of grass you have. Generally, it’s best to mow your lawn to a height of 2-3 inches. Taller grass shades the soil, which can help to retain moisture and prevent weeds from growing.
  • How to mow: Use a sharp mower blade to ensure a clean cut, and mow in a different direction each time to avoid creating ruts in your lawn. Be sure to leave the grass clippings on your lawn, as they can provide valuable nutrients.

Tip #6: Control Weeds

Weeds can be a nuisance in your lawn, as they can crowd out your grass and make your lawn look unkempt.

  • Prevention: The best way to control weeds is to prevent them from growing in the first place. This can be done by maintaining a healthy lawn through proper watering, fertilization, and mowing. You can also use a pre-emergent herbicide to prevent weed seeds from germinating.
  • Removal: If you already have weeds in your lawn, it’s important to remove them before they spread. You can remove weeds by hand, using a tool such as a weed digger or hoe, or you can use a post-emergent herbicide to kill the weeds.
  • Maintenance: Regular maintenance of your lawn can also help to control weeds. Be sure to remove any weeds as soon as you see them before they have a chance to spread.

Tip #7: Treat Lawn Diseases and Pests

Diseases and pests can wreak havoc on your lawn, causing damage and making it less attractive.

  • How to identify problems: Some signs that your lawn may be experiencing a disease or pest problem include yellowing or browning grass, thinning or dead patches, and the presence of insects or other pests.
  • How to treat diseases: If you suspect that your lawn has a disease, it’s important to identify the specific problem before treating it. There are many different types of lawn diseases, and each one requires a different treatment approach. You can consult with a lawn care professional or your local gardening center for guidance on how to treat specific diseases.
  • How to treat pests: If your lawn has a pest problem, you can use insecticides or other treatments to control the pests. Again, it’s important to identify the specific pest before treating it, as different pests require different approaches. You can consult with a lawn care professional or your local gardening center for guidance on how to treat specific pests.

Tip #8: Use Landscaping to Enhance Your Lawn

Choose the Right Plants

When selecting plants for your landscaping, it’s important to choose species that are well-suited to your climate and soil type. Native plants are often a good choice, as they tend to be more adapted to local conditions.

Create Focal Points

A focal point can draw the eye and add interest to your lawn. This can be a statue, a fountain, or even a well-placed tree.

Use Hardscaping Elements

Hardscaping elements like paths, walls, and seating areas can add structure and definition to your lawn. They can also create a sense of unity between different areas of your outdoor space.

Incorporate Lighting

Outdoor lighting can add ambiance to your lawn, as well as make it more functional and safe at night.

Tip #9: Take Care of Your Lawn Equipment

  • Keep your equipment clean: After each use, take the time to clean your lawn equipment thoroughly. This will help prevent rust and other damage, as well as ensure that your equipment lasts as long as possible.
  • Change the oil: If you have gas-powered equipment, it’s important to change the oil regularly. This will keep your engine running smoothly and prevent damage.
  • Sharpen blades: Dull blades can damage your lawn and create an uneven cut. Be sure to sharpen your mower blades regularly to ensure a clean cut and promote healthy growth.
  • Store equipment properly: When you’re not using your lawn equipment, be sure to store it in a dry, covered area. This will help prevent damage from moisture, sunlight, and other environmental factors.

Tip #10: Hire a Professional Lawn Care Service

Maintaining a beautiful and healthy lawn can be a lot of work, and if you don’t have the time or expertise to do it yourself, hiring a professional lawn care service can be a great option. By hiring a professional, you can free up your time for other activities and ensure that your lawn receives regular and consistent maintenance, resulting in a healthier and more attractive yard. Professional lawn care services also have access to specialized equipment and supplies that can lead to better results.
