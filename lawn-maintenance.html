Local Conditions

Why Does Yard Maintenance Take More Work in New Orleans?

Yard maintenance takes more work in New Orleans because intense heat, humidity, and heavy Gulf rains keep lawns, weeds, and beds growing for most of the year. Grass greens up early and grows fast all summer, weeds set seed quickly, and storms leave debris behind, so a yard left alone for a few weeks falls behind.

  • The growing season is long.In south Louisiana, warm-season lawns can start greening up in late February and grow fast all summer.
  • Weeds return from seed.LSU AgCenter advises removing weeds before they drop seeds, which means fewer weeds the following season.
  • Mulch earns its keep.A mulch layer blocks light from weed seeds and helps beds hold water through hot, dry spells.
Seasonal Care

What Does a Seasonal Lawn Maintenance Program Cover in New Orleans?

A seasonal lawn maintenance program in New Orleans shifts its work with the year: mowing restarts and beds are refreshed in spring, weekly cuts and weed control carry the summer, mulch is topped up in fall, and leaf cleanup and hauling take over while the grass rests in winter.

Spring

March to May

Mowing restarts as lawns green up. Beds are cleared of winter debris, and mulch is checked for thin spots.

Summer

June to August

Weekly mowing and edging through the fastest growth, with weeds cut back before they set seed.

Fall

September to November

Visits spread out as growth slows. Mulch around shrubs is replenished to 2 to 4 inches deep.

Winter

December to February

Grass is mostly dormant, so visits turn to raking leaves, clearing beds, and hauling yard waste away.

Season timing and mulch depth follow LSU AgCenter guidance. Mulch is kept off tree trunks, where it traps moisture against the bark.

FAQ

What Do People Ask About Lawn Maintenance in New Orleans?

what is lawn maintenance?

Lawn maintenance is the regular care that keeps a yard healthy and tidy: mowing at the right height, edging, trimming, weed control, and keeping beds and hard surfaces clean. In New Orleans, where grass grows for most of the year, it usually runs on a weekly or every-other-week schedule during the peak season.

what does lawn maintenance include?

A lawn maintenance visit usually includes mowing, edging along walks and drives, trimming in spots a mower cannot reach, and clearing clippings from hard surfaces. Our plans can also add weed cutting, mulching for beds, pressure washing, and clean up and hauling, so a single visit can cover the whole yard.

what does yard maintenance include?

Yard maintenance covers more than the lawn. Along with mowing and trimming, it covers planting beds and mulch, weeds along fences and walks, fallen leaves and yard debris, and hard surfaces such as patios and driveways. Booked as one program, those services run together on one schedule.

how much does lawn maintenance cost?

The cost of lawn maintenance depends on the size of the property, which services each visit includes, and how often the work is scheduled. A weekly program with mulching and cleanups costs more than a basic mow. The most accurate number comes from a free, no-obligation estimate based on your actual yard.

how much is lawn maintenance per month?

The monthly cost of lawn maintenance comes down to the number of visits in that month and what each visit covers. In New Orleans, summer months usually need more visits than winter months, when warm-season grass is mostly dormant. We quote your plan after a free look at the property, with no obligation to book.

what does regular lawn maintenance involve?

Regular lawn maintenance involves mowing on a steady schedule without removing more than a third of the grass blade at once, plus edging, trimming, and keeping weeds from seeding. Through the year it also means topping up mulch as it thins, clearing leaves and storm debris, and adjusting visits as growth speeds up or slows.

what is the best lawn maintenance company?

The best lawn maintenance company for your yard is one that is insured, gives a clear quote before starting, sets the schedule around your grass and the season, and stands behind its work. Read local reviews too. We offer free estimates and a satisfaction guarantee, and every job goes to a fully insured professional.

who is responsible for lawn maintenance, landlord or tenant?

Responsibility for lawn maintenance depends on the lease, which may put routine mowing and upkeep on the tenant or leave it with the landlord or a property manager. Check the lease wording first, and ask the landlord in writing if anything is unclear. We offer maintenance plans for both residential and commercial properties.
