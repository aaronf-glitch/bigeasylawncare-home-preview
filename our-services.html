What Landscaping Services Do We Provide in New Orleans?

Our New Orleans landscaping team builds new landscapes and restores tired ones. That covers landscape building and restoration, plus the systems that keep a yard working here: irrigation for dry spells, drainage for heavy Gulf rains, outdoor lighting, and garden beds planted for local heat and humidity.

A landscape in New Orleans has to handle both extremes, weeks of heat and then a storm that drops inches of rain at once. We plan the layout, the plants, and the water around that, so the yard still looks right after the weather has had its turn.

Hardscape

What Hardscape Work Do We Build in New Orleans Yards?

Our hardscape work in New Orleans gives a yard its structure: stone paving, patios, courtyard installation, and fire and water features. Hardscape is the part of a yard that stays put through every season, so it sets where people walk, sit, and gather long before the plants fill in around it.

Paving and patios also take pressure off the lawn. A path where everyone already walks saves the grass from wearing thin, and a courtyard turns a shady corner where turf struggles into space you can actually use.

What Outdoor Living Features Do We Install in New Orleans?

We install outdoor living features that let New Orleans families use the yard through long, hot summers: pergolas and decks, outdoor kitchens and outdoor bars, pond installation, and cooling and misting systems. FAQ

What Do People Ask About Lawn Care Services in New Orleans?

What lawn care services does Big Easy Lawn Care offer?

We offer four groups of services in New Orleans: lawn and yard maintenance, landscaping, hardscape, and outdoor living. That runs from grass cutting, trimming, mulching, weed cutting, pressure washing, and cleanup to landscape building and restoration, patios and paving, and pergolas, decks, and outdoor kitchens.

What is included in lawn and yard maintenance?

Lawn and yard maintenance covers grass or lawn cutting, lawn trimming, lawn mulching, weed cutting, pressure washing, and clean up and hauling. You can book one of them as a single visit, or set up an ongoing maintenance program that combines regular cuts with seasonal yard cleanups through the year.

Do you install landscaping as well as maintain lawns?

Yes. Beyond regular maintenance, our landscaping team handles landscape building and landscape restoration, along with irrigation, drainage, outdoor lighting, and garden beds. Each project is planned around New Orleans heat, humidity, and heavy rain, so the new landscape holds up after the first hard storm of the season.

Do you build patios and other hardscape features?

Yes. Our hardscape work includes stone paving, patios, courtyard installation, and fire and water features. Hardscape gives a yard its structure and takes foot traffic off the lawn, which helps in shady New Orleans yards where grass struggles to hold under live oaks and heavy use.

Can you build a pergola or a deck?

Yes. Pergolas and decks are part of our outdoor living services, along with outdoor kitchens, outdoor bars, pond installation, and cooling and misting systems. We plan each one around how you want to use the yard, especially shade and airflow for long, hot New Orleans summers.

Do you offer one-time cleanups and weed cutting?

Yes. You can book one-time mowing and weed-eating for an overgrown yard, a single weed cutting visit, or a seasonal cleanup with hauling. Many customers start with one visit, see the result, and then add an ongoing maintenance schedule so the yard never gets that far ahead again.

Do you provide lawn services for commercial properties?

Yes. We serve both residential and commercial properties across the greater New Orleans area, with plans built around the size of the site and how it is used. Businesses can book the same maintenance, landscaping, hardscape, and outdoor living services that homeowners use.

How much do lawn care services cost in New Orleans?

The cost depends on the size of the property, the services involved, and how often you need them. Every New Orleans yard is different, so the most accurate number comes from a free on-site estimate, which we provide with no obligation before any work is scheduled.
