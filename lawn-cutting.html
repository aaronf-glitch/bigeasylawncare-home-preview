Local Conditions

Why Does Grass Cutting Work Differently in New Orleans?

Grass cutting works differently in New Orleans because our warm-season grasses grow fast through a long, hot, humid season, many Orleans Parish yards sit under heavy shade, and summer storms can soak a lawn for days. A schedule copied from a cooler climate cuts too seldom in July and too short under the oaks.

  • The season runs long.Lawns green up in March, and summer heat and storms speed growth, especially on Bermuda.
  • Shade changes the height.St. Augustine under live oaks can be kept at 3 to 3.5 inches to catch more light.
  • Rain changes the day.LSU AgCenter advises against mowing wet grass, so after a storm we wait for the lawn to dry before we cut.
Mowing Heights

What Mowing Height Is Best for Grass in New Orleans?

The best mowing height for New Orleans grass depends on the species. St. Augustine, one of the most common local lawns, does best at 2.5 to 3 inches in sun and 3 to 3.5 inches in shade, while Zoysia and Centipede sit at 1 to 2 inches and Bermuda can go as low as 0.75 inches.

St. Augustine

2.5 to 3 in

The local default and one of the best warm-season grasses for shade. Raise it to 3 to 3.5 inches under trees.

Zoysia

1 to 2 in

A dense, fine-to-medium turf that handles foot traffic and partial shade, and gets noticeably better in years two and three.

Bermuda

0.75 to 2 in

Grows fast, recovers from wear quickly, and needs full sun, so it suits open play areas and calls for regular summer cuts.

Centipede

1 to 2 in

Needs less feeding, grows slowly, and tolerates acidic soils. Heavy fertilizing and foot traffic both work against it.

Mowing heights follow LSU AgCenter guidance. Whatever the grass, we never take off more than a third of the blade in one cut.

FAQ

What Do People Ask About Grass Cutting in New Orleans?

how often should you mow your lawn in New Orleans?

In New Orleans, mow by height rather than the calendar. Cut whenever the grass reaches about one and a half times its target height, which usually means every 5 to 7 days in spring, weekly or more in summer, every 10 to 14 days by late fall, and rarely in winter.

what is the best time of day to mow the lawn?

Mid-morning, once the dew has dried and before the afternoon heat builds, is a good time to mow a New Orleans lawn. The grass is dry enough to cut cleanly, and the turf avoids the stress of being cut in the hottest part of a summer afternoon.

can you mow a wet lawn?

It is best not to. LSU AgCenter advises against mowing wet grass, because the clippings clump on the surface and wet blades tend to tear instead of cutting cleanly. After a New Orleans storm, wait until the lawn has dried, then mow at the normal height without trying to catch up.

how short should you cut your grass?

How short to cut depends on the grass: St. Augustine does best at 2.5 to 3 inches, or 3 to 3.5 inches in shade, while Zoysia and Centipede sit at 1 to 2 inches. Whatever the species, never remove more than one third of the blade in a single cut.

should you leave grass clippings on the lawn?

Usually, yes. LSU AgCenter notes that properly managed clippings supply nutrients as they decompose and do not add to thatch buildup. Clippings should be removed when they form clumps, which mostly happens when grass is cut wet or allowed to grow too tall.

when do you stop mowing in New Orleans?

Mowing slows through the fall and mostly stops from late November to February, when warm-season grass goes dormant. St. Augustine may stay partly green in a mild winter, so a light pass to clear leaves and keep the yard tidy is usually all a New Orleans lawn needs.

how much does lawn mowing cost in New Orleans?

The cost of lawn mowing in New Orleans depends on the size of the lawn, how often it is cut, and what each visit includes, such as edging or a cleanup. The most accurate number comes from a free on-site estimate, which we provide with no obligation.

how long does it take to mow a lawn?

It depends on the size of the yard, the grass, how tall it has grown, and how much edging the property needs. A regularly maintained lawn takes far less time than an overgrown one, and we give you a clear schedule with the estimate before the first cut.
